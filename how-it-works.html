How i ii Living Works

End-to-end process to rent or subscribe to a middle-range car in Malaysia.

01

Choose your plan

Select daily rental, monthly subscription, or yearly commitment based on your use case.

02

Submit documents

Upload IC/passport, valid driving license, and complete simple identity verification.

03

Approve & reserve

We review your request and confirm availability, price, and pickup location.

04

Pay securely

Make payment with card or online banking. Receive invoice and e-contract instantly.

05

Collect your car

Pickup from selected hub or opt for delivery in eligible service zones.

06

Manage online

Track booking, extensions, receipts, and support tickets from your client portal.
